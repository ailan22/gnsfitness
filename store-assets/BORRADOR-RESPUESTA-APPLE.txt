2. Purpose and audience
GNS FITNESS supports the relationship between the GNS FITNESS gym and its members.
Members can view assigned workouts, record exercise load and repetitions in reserve,
review their progress and membership information, and request nutrition support.
Authorized staff manage member records, plans, payments recorded by the gym, workouts,
and appointments. Health intake and rehabilitation records are restricted to the
administrator. The app is not a public social network and members do not browse
other members' personal records.

3. Access and main features
There is no open self-registration. Gym administration creates/invites member access.
Sign in with the demo credentials supplied through App Review Information, then open
the assigned workout and its expandable exercise fields. Nutrition includes a request
and approval flow; enabled members can view the educational guides, recipe library
and their assigned nutrition plan. Biometric access is optional on supported devices.

4. External services
Supabase provides authentication, PostgreSQL data storage and the invitation Edge
Function. Cloudflare hosts the web version and public support/privacy pages.
Capacitor packages the iPhone client; the biometric plugin uses the device's biometric
capabilities and protected credential storage. No face images or biometric templates
are sent to the application server. Codemagic is used to build/sign/upload releases.
The operator keeps encrypted recovery copies in Google Drive through a separate
backup process. There is no integrated card processor or Apple in-app purchasing SDK.
The routine text importer parses the supplied text; it does not send it to an external
generative AI service. Automatic WhatsApp delivery is not yet connected.
